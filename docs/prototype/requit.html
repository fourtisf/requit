Follow the money

Outside money in. Your money out.

Nothing here is funded by members, deposits, or token sales. The money enters at step one, and it comes from companies buying customers.

3

The gap between what the advertiser pays and what you're paid. It runs the company and fills the vault.

Questions

Straight answers.

How much can I actually earn?

Surveys land around $2–6 an hour when you qualify, and you will sometimes be screened out after five minutes with nothing. Game offers pay more in total but worse per hour, because the deepest tier is usually out of reach inside the deadline. We publish the real completion rate so you can judge before you commit three weeks.

Do I need $RQT to earn?

No. Tasks pay USD or ETH to anyone eligible. Holding the token adds a second lane. It never gates the first one and it never improves your task rates.

What if an offer doesn't track?

File a dispute with the offer name, date and your screenshots. We forward it to the network and show you the status. Most resolve in 5–14 days. Some don't, which is why we tell you to always start from the Requit link and keep evidence as you go.

Why do some offers require a purchase?

Certain advertisers only pay on a deposit or in-app purchase. Those are labeled in amber with the exact amount before you open them. We don't treat them as defaults and we don't recommend spending to chase a milestone — that's how the offer stops being work and starts being a cost.

Which wallets do I need?

Either or both. A Solana wallet receives USD. An EVM wallet on Base receives ETH, holds $RQT and is what the Sunday snapshot reads. Linking signs a free message that cannot move funds or approve spending.

Is $RQT an investment?

No. It is a volatile token on a public pool. Distributions depend on volume, are discretionary, and can be paused. It can lose all of its value. Requit does not sell it and does not give investment advice.
